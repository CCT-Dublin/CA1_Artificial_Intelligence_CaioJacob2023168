## Project Overview

This repository contains the research and development work for Continuous Assessment 1 (CA1) for the Artificial Intelligence module.

The project focuses on **Computer Vision**, with particular emphasis on **Facial Recognition Technology (FRT)** and its use in law enforcement.

The research examines the following question:

> **What are the ethical, legal and social impacts of facial recognition technology, and how can these impacts be mitigated while maximising its benefits?**

The **Robert Williams case and the Detroit Police Department** were selected as the main real-world case study. The case demonstrates how an incorrect facial recognition result, combined with insufficient independent verification, can lead to serious consequences for an individual.

The project considers both the potential benefits of facial recognition and the risks associated with accuracy, privacy, fairness, human oversight, accountability and public trust.

## Research Focus

The project is structured around four main areas:

### 1. Computer Vision and Facial Recognition

The project explains the fundamentals of Computer Vision and how facial recognition systems can process images, detect faces, extract features and compare biometric representations.

The research distinguishes between:

- **1:1 Verification** – comparing one person against one stored identity.
- **1:N Identification** – comparing an input face against a larger biometric gallery to produce potential candidate matches.

### 2. Ethical, Legal and Social Impacts

The research examines:

- Accuracy and reliability
- Demographic differences in algorithmic performance
- Automation bias and human decision-making
- Privacy and biometric data
- GDPR and European AI regulation
- Accountability and human oversight
- Public trust and social consequences

### 3. Real-World Case Study

The main case study is **Robert Williams and the Detroit Police Department**.

The research examines how a facial recognition result was used as an investigative lead, the subsequent investigation and photo line-up, and the wrongful arrest and detention of Robert Williams in January 2020.

The project also examines the policy changes resulting from the 2024 settlement.

### 4. Mitigation and Responsible Use

The project identifies technical and operational approaches for reducing risks, including:

- Image quality assessment
- Independent verification
- Demographic performance testing
- Human oversight
- Staff training
- Auditing and accountability
